# Roadmap

- Where cameraif and CircuitPython's `espcamera` do the same job, cameraif
  takes `espcamera`'s API: taking a picture, flipping and mirroring, the
  pixel formats, reconfiguring. What only a MIPI-CSI camera has stays as is.

Bugs, and things you need that don't work yet, go to
[issues](https://github.com/PyDevices/cameraif/issues).
